# No timers, lives or game over

- **Date:** 2026-09-26
- **Status:** Decided
- **Decided by:** agent, from the research, and carried into the user's CLAUDE.md rules
- **Revisit when:** New research shows time pressure is harmless at ages 4–6, or the target age range moves up.

## Decision
No timers, lives, game over or speed drills. A wrong answer shows a hint model (the dots counted aloud), and then the child retries. The arcade feel comes from movement and the dragon, not from pressure.

## Why
Math anxiety appears as early as 1st–2nd grade and is linked to lower achievement, and adults' anxiety spreads to children. Speed pressure is exactly what the original Math Blaster relied on, so this is the main thing we change from it. See design rule 5 in the research.

## Rejected
- **Timed rounds or lives, as in Math Blaster:** the anxiety risk above.
- **Eggs drifting off-screen:** considered while building Egg Zapper, and rejected because it creates time pressure. The eggs bob in place instead.
