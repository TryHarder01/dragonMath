Part 2 · Game design · Ages 4–6

Ember's
Egg Rescue

A storm scattered the Dino Nest's eggs. You ride Ember, a kind young dragon, to find them, warm them, count the babies and bring everyone home. Every finished round hatches a new friend.

It has Math Blaster's arcade loop and hero mission, rebuilt around how 4–6 year olds actually learn number. Nothing is timed and you can't lose.

From Math Blaster

What we keep and what we drop

Keep

  • A hero with a mission (Ember and the Nest)
  • The arcade loop: tap the right answer and see something happen
  • A map of mini-games to choose from
  • Collectibles that make you want one more round

Drop

  • Countdown timers and falling-too-fast pressure
  • Zapping and blasting. Ember only helps
  • Lives, health bars and game over
  • Bare flashcard numerals with no picture
  • Written instructions (players can't read yet)
Core loop

Five problems, then something hatches

Island map→ Pick a mini-game→ 5 problems→ Egg hatches→ Back to map

Right answer

A sparkle burst and praise that names the strategy: "You counted every egg!"

Wrong answer

A gentle "hmm". The dots light up one at a time while the voice counts them, that choice greys out, and the child tries again. No buzzer.

Six mini-games · one learning trajectory each

The island

🥚Subitizing · numeral ↔ quantity

Egg Warmer

"Warm the egg with four!"

Chilly eggs show dice dots, then ten-frames, then numerals. Ember's warm glow helps the right one hatch.

L1 dots 1–3 → L5 ten-frame vs. numerals to 10
🦕One-to-one · cardinality

Dino Count

"How many dinos?"

Tap each baby once while the voice counts along, then pick the total. At level 5 it flips to "Give me 7."

L1 1–3 in a row → L5 count out N
💎Comparing

Gem Trade

"Who has more gems?"

Two dragons hold gem piles. The gap between them shrinks each level, from 1 vs. 5 down to 7 vs. 8. "Fewer" questions start at level 3.

L1 lined-up rows → L5 numerals only
🦖Number line · counting on

Stomp Path

"Four, five, six!"

A T-rex stomps down a straight numbered path. Spin, then tap each square as the voice says it. This is the Siegler & Ramani board game as a mini-game.

L1 1–10 path → L5 1–20, predict where you'll land
🪺Composing · make 5 and 10

Nest Builder

"How many more to fill the nest?"

The nest is a ten-frame. Tap eggs in to fill it, then later answer with just the number.

L1 five-frame → L5 "make 7"
📖Add & subtract

Dino Story

"Three dinos splash. Two more stomp over!"

Animated stories where dinos actually walk in and fly off. Every answer choice shows a numeral with its dots.

L1 join within 5 → L5 missing part within 10
Adaptive difficulty · per mini-game

Always one step ahead of the child

3 in a row

correct answers moves that mini-game up a level.

2 misses

on problems in a row quietly moves it back down. There's no "level down" message.

~80%

success rate is the target: hard enough to stretch them without being discouraging.

Rewards

The Hatchery

About 20 babies to collect, each with a name, like "Pip the Purple Raptor". One hatches after every finished round, never in the middle of a problem, so the reward doesn't pull attention away from the math.
